# Personal Preferences & Appearance

Each household member can customize their experience on Tilora without affecting other users.

---

## Appearance & Theming

Navigate to **Settings → Your Settings → Appearance**:

Tilora includes several built-in themes optimized for both low-light and bright ambient environments:

- **Dark** (Default): Sleek dark background with vibrant accent colors. Ideal for night-time and OLED displays.
- **Light**: Crisp, high-contrast light theme for bright daylight rooms.
- **Sepia**: Warm, low-blue-light tone reminiscent of paper.
- **High-Contrast**: Maximum contrast typography and borders for high-glare environments and accessibility.

---

## Language & Internationalization

Navigate to **Settings → Your Settings → Language**:

Tilora provides full multi-language support across the backend and frontend:

- 🇬🇧 **English** (`en`, default)
- 🇪🇸 **Español** (`es`)
- 🇫🇷 **Français** (`fr`)
- 🇩🇪 **Deutsch** (`de`)

Changing your language updates all dashboard labels, dates, weather forecasts, error messages, and widget descriptions in real time.

---

## Location Preferences

Navigate to **Settings → Your Settings → Location**:

By default, widgets (such as Weather, Flights, and Mapping) use the household coordinates set in `dashboard.yaml`. You can set a personal location override:

1. Type a city name in the **Search for a city…** field.
2. Select your city from the search results.
3. Tap **Save location**.

Your location override will be used for your profile's weather and mapping calculations across all devices.

---

## Device Management

Navigate to **Settings → Your Settings → Devices**:

- **Rename this device**: Give the screen you are currently looking at a distinct name (e.g. `"Office Monitor"` or `"iPad Air"`).
- **Forget device** (Admin only): Remove older, retired devices from your household registry.
